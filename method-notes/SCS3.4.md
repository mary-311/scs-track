A three-tab structure (Lists, Records, Dashboard) keeps reference data, append-only events, and summary formulas separated.
Data validation set to reject invalid input prevents bad item names, invalid dates, and non-positive quantities at entry.
Protecting formula cells and the Dashboard stops accidental overwrites while keeping entry columns usable.
Hand-checking formulas against partner-picked rows and month boundaries catches formula or data-type mistakes early.
Recording mistakes as correcting rows preserves a clear audit trail without silently rewriting history.
What I might be wrong about: the kiosk owner may type entries faster on a phone where dropdowns or date formats feel slow, or may still overwrite cells despite guidance—I would find out during live check-ins and version history reviews.

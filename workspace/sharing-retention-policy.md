## 1. Where files live
All active business records, invoices, and staff rosters live in Google Shared Drives owned by the business under `/Trainees/belisha`, never in individual personal My Drive folders.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is restricted to warning or blocking outside domains by default (`Apps > Google Workspace > Drive and Docs > Sharing settings`). Only designated managers may share specific view-only links with verified suppliers.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active staff with company-issued accounts in our organizational unit may sign in. Two-Step Verification (2SV) is enforced for all staff and admin accounts (`Security > Authentication > 2-step verification`), with paper backup codes stored securely offline.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Operational spreadsheets, receipts, and client agreements are retained in Shared Drives. Deleted files remain in Drive Trash for 30 days before permanent removal; Drive admin restore can recover permanently deleted items for up to 25 additional days.

## 5. The monthly export
On the last Friday of every month, the designated admin runs a Google Takeout export of core business Shared Drive folders and stores the archive on an encrypted offline backup drive.

## 6. Who holds admin
Only the business owner and one delegated IT administrator hold Super Admin or delegated admin privileges. Daily operational work is performed using standard non-admin user accounts.

## 7. When someone leaves
When a staff member leaves, their account is immediately suspended (`Directory > Users > Suspend user`), their Drive ownership is transferred to their manager, 2SV/app sessions are revoked, and their data is exported before account deletion.
